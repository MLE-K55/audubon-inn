OUR FEATURED OUTING

Meet the marsh.

Bring your binoculars and leave room to wander. Horicon Marsh offers birdwatching, walking, wildlife photography, and a closer look at Wisconsin’s wetland landscape.

Explore the national wildlife refuge’s trails and visitor information, or discover exhibits and programs at the Horicon Marsh Education & Visitor Center. Check current access before choosing your route.
