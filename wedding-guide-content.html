01 / YOUR GUESTS

Nine rooms or eighteen?

A nine-room block can bring your closest family, friends, or wedding party together. An eighteen-room block can keep your group in all of the inn’s guest rooms. Both are subject to availability for your dates.

  • Estimate rooms by household, rather than treating each guest as a separate room.
  • Confirm the exact room allocation, guest-booking instructions, payment responsibilities, and release dates.
  • Ask whether a minimum stay applies and how changes or cancellations work.
  • Confirm any exclusive use of the hotel or public spaces in writing.

Explore room styles and arrival details before making your list.

02 / YOUR CELEBRATION

Picture the gathering.

The event space can accommodate up to 150 people, with around 70 for a comfortable gathering. Your ideal layout depends on tables, seating, service, dancing, and the way you want to celebrate.

Request a tour with your guest count in mind. Discuss ceremony and reception plans, private bar service, food arrangements, supplier access, music, setup and cleanup, and the event end time. Confirm what is included in your rental.

03 / THE TIME AROUND THE DAY

Leave room for the weekend.

Before the celebration

Share arrival instructions and a room-block booking link with your guests. Check-in starts after 2 p.m. If you need a room for earlier preparations, discuss access or a previous-night stay in advance.

Time together

Choose time for a welcome gathering, your celebration, and an unhurried goodbye. Ask about private bar service; the Beaumont’s public hours are Friday and Saturday, 5–10 p.m., subject to private-event closures.

The next morning

Checkout is at 10 a.m. Share that timing with your group, and point guests toward local dining or a marsh outing if they have time to explore.

Pets are welcome for an extra fee. Guests traveling with a pet should confirm the fee and their room with the inn before booking.

04 / CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Know what you’re booking.

Your written quote should make the full arrangement clear. Check the space and timing, food and bar service, staffing, furniture and linens, setup and cleanup, guest-room commitments, deposits, taxes and other charges, and cancellation terms. These are questions to review, rather than promises that every item is included.

Ask about the entrance route, steps or elevator, bathrooms, parking, and unloading for any guests or suppliers who need a particular arrangement.

05 / START THE CONVERSATION

Your first message.

  • Preferred date and a backup date
  • Wedding plans and estimated guest count
  • Nine or eighteen guest rooms, and your expected nights
  • Food and private bar service needs
  • Interest in a tour and any access requirements

The team will discuss availability, fit, pricing, and the next steps. Your date and rooms are confirmed through your agreement.
