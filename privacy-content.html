How this website connects you with the inn and its booking and event services.

Rooms and event requests

Room availability and reservations open on ResNexus. Event inquiries are prepared locally in your browser. Use your email app to send the draft to the inn, or call the front desk. Preparing a draft does not send a message. The inn receives it only after you send it.

This website does not collect payment details or submit an inquiry in the background. Calling or emailing uses the phone or email application you choose.

Optional analytics

{{ANALYTICS_STATUS}}

If enabled in the future, optional Google Analytics will load after you choose to allow it. It can measure page views and actions such as opening room availability, an event inquiry, directions, or a phone link. A link click is not a confirmed reservation, sent inquiry, or completed event booking.

Inquiry fields are not saved in browser storage or sent by this website to a form service. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, message text, and reservation details are not included in the website’s analytics events. Shared page locations exclude query strings and fragments. Analytics uses cookies only when allowed; you can change your choice from the website footer when analytics is configured.

Your browser and the website host

A privacy choice, when applicable, is saved in your browser’s local storage. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information as described in its privacy statement. External links, maps, and booking providers have their own policies.

Questions or changes to a booking

Contact the inn at hotelonthemarsh@gmail.com or 920-644-2087. For a reservation change, contact the inn rather than replying through an analytics or website feature.
