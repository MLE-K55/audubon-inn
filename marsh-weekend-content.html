ONE MARSH. DIFFERENT STARTING POINTS.

Choose your route first.

Horicon National Wildlife Refuge covers the northern part of the marsh; the state wildlife area is a separate destination. Choose your visitor center or trail before you get directions.

  • National refuge visitor center: W4279 Headquarters Road, Mayville. See the official refuge visitor guide for current facilities and access.
  • Horicon Marsh Education & Visitor Center: N7725 State Highway 28, Horicon. Find programs, exhibits, and visitor information at Friends of Horicon Marsh.

Check the specific destination’s current hours and route, particularly during seasonal closures. Do not use a single distance or drive time for every part of the marsh.

AN ITINERARY YOU CAN MAKE YOUR OWN

A slower weekend.

Arrive and settle in.

Check in at the inn after 2 p.m. Take time to unpack and choose a local table for dinner. On Friday and Saturday, the Beaumont Bar opens 5–10 p.m.; check for private-event closures.

Make the morning yours.

Pack layers, water, binoculars, and your camera. Start with current visitor information, then choose an outing that suits your group and the weather. Leave time to stop, listen, and watch rather than trying to cover every route.

Return for a local evening.

Head back to Mayville for a meal and another night at the inn. Check our local calendar for community programs that may fit your visit.

Leave unhurried.

Checkout is at 10 a.m. Keep your final morning simple or plan an outing after checking out.

BEFORE YOU HEAD OUT

Good details make a better day.

Use official maps, check the forecast and access notices, and choose footwear for the conditions. Wildlife sightings vary. Follow posted rules and give birds and other wildlife space.

The refuge’s Egret Trail boardwalk does not allow dogs; other public-use routes have their own rules. If your pet is traveling with you, check each destination before leaving the inn.
